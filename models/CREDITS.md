# Imported 3D models

Converted from the owner's downloaded model collection: FBX/OBJ converted to GLB, textures fixed,
resized to at most 1024 px WebP, meshes simplified where very dense, meshopt-compressed (about 400 MB in, 20 MB out).
Original authors and licences come with the source downloads; check them before publishing the game.
